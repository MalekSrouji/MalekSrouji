### AI and research

#### Federated outbreak detection (master's thesis)

Combines social media, search-engine and hospital data through federated fusion, so each source trains on its own side. On a 243-day COVID-19 case study it gave a steady early warning 79 days before the benchmark date, and a high-confidence signal 24 days before it. *Code being cleaned up for release.*

#### Wi-Fi CSI sensing (IEEE conference paper, co-author)

Telling empty, organic and metallic environments apart using Wi-Fi channel state information. We compared classical and deep models and checked with explainability tools that predictions came from real radio features. TabNet reached 96.0% accuracy, macro-AUC 0.993.
